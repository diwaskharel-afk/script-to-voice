# Reports dashboard

Sign in and open **Reports**. The dashboard shows the last 7 days by default.

## Date range
Use the date picker in the top right. Choose *Last 30 days* for a monthly view.

## Filters
Click **Add filter**, choose a field such as **Region**, then pick one or more values (for example EMEA).

## Export
Open the **Export** menu and choose **CSV** or **PDF**. CSV exports include every row, not just the visible page.

## Scheduling
Click **Schedule** to email the report automatically. Choose daily, weekly or monthly, pick the day and time, and save.
